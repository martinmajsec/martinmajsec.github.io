About me
==========

I am a computer science student at the [Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing](https://www.fer.unizg.hr/en). 
As part of [MODISTe](https://www.fer.unizg.hr/modiste), 
I completed my [BSc thesis](https://repozitorij.fer.unizg.hr/islandora/object/fer:13985) on an open problem on spanning trees
under the supervision of prof. dr. sc. Anamari Nakić. 

### Research interests

My core interest is studying how trade-offs between computational resources and solution quality can make hard problems easier to solve by better reflecting realistic settings.

This includes approximation algorithms, restricted computational models such as streaming and memory-bounded computation, and computational complexity, all of which study how different structural assumptions affect what can be computed efficiently.
In the future, I am also looking to expand this to domain-specific programming languages and computer architecture.


Furthermore, one of my favorite mental exercises is programming language and compiler design. 
Outside of my studies, I have contributed to Virtue, a programming language by Zorislav Shoyat that enables automatic parallel program execution, and I am currently working on code augmentation via static analysis for 
[Apertus](https://apertvs.ai/), a SwissAI LLM. 


I am also interested in formalizing notions that haven't been thoroughly explored in mathematics, such as the [Conveyer-Belt Problem](https://erikdemaine.org/papers/ConveyerAlphabet_Elasticity/).

### Competitive programming

Alongside my studies, I teach competitive programming to elementary and high school students at [Zagrebački Računalni Savez](https://zrs.hr/), a lively community of computer enthusiasts.
Over the course of five years, I have regularly mentored over 20 students in groups of 4–8. 
The students primarily train for the state competitions Infokup and [COCI](https://hsin.hr/coci/announcement.html), and later on for international programming competitions such as the [EJOI](https://www.ejoi2026.lmio.lt/) and the [IOI](https://ioinformatics.org/).
